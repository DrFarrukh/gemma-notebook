### 1. What is Ex-Pakistan Leave (EPL)?
Ex-Pakistan Leave (EPL) is a specific category of leave granted to employees who apply for it or proceed abroad during their leave period or take leave while on duty abroad [C4]. It must be requested specifically by the employee [C4].

### 2. Can you take EPL if you are on probation?
**No.** You cannot take Ex-Pakistan Leave (EPL) if you are currently on probation.
*   **Evidence:** The policy explicitly states that to avail Extraordinary Leave (EOL)—which is the category governing long-term leave abroad including EPL—the employee must have been removed from probation [C6].

### 3. Is EPL "Leave with Pay" or "Leave without Pay"?
The payment status depends on the specific circumstances and funding source:
*   **With Pay:** If the study (or leave) is fully or partially funded by the University, a bond/undertaking to serve NUST upon completion is required [C1]. The policy mentions that EPL can be granted "with/without pay" depending on authorization [C12].
*   **Without Pay:** If the employee proceeds abroad without specific university funding covering the period, it may be treated as leave without pay (EOL), which generally does not accrue increments or end-of-service benefits [C6].
*   **Duration Limits:** EPL is subject to a maximum of thirty (30) days at a time or part thereof, based on the available balance of Earned Leave (EL) [C4]. It cannot exceed the earned leave balance up to a maximum of 30 days [C4].

# Q4

What are the min and max faculty loads for an associate professor?

Based on the supplied evidence, the minimum and maximum faculty loads for an Associate Professor (Assoc Prof) depend on their status regarding PhD qualification, tenure track system participation, and specific roles such as supervision or institutional service. The following details are extracted from the Faculty Load Policy documents [C1], [C8], [C9], [C11].

### 1. Minimum Teaching Load
The minimum mandatory teaching load is calculated based on a base requirement minus research and institutional service loads.

*   **General Rule:** For faculty members, the formula is:  
    `Minimum Teaching Load = 12 CHs – Research Load – Institutional Services` [C1].
*   **Newly Joined PhD Faculty:** For newly joined PhD faculty (which includes Associate Professors joining after a PhD), the base requirement is lower:  
    `Minimum Teaching Load = 8 CHs – Research Load – Institutional Services` [C1].
*   **Specific Role Adjustments:** Certain roles have fixed loads that may affect the teaching load calculation. For example, Heads of Departments (HoDs) and Journal Editors in Chief (EIC) are assigned a specific **3 CH** institutional service load [C9].

### 2. Maximum Faculty Load (Overall)
The overall faculty load varies based on the type of semester and the individual's role or research output.

*   **Regular Semester:**  
    The minimum overall load for a regular semester is generally around **12 CHs** (including teaching, research, and institutional services) [C8]. However, this can be higher if specific roles or research activities are involved.
*   **Summer Semester:**  
    Only faculty members who have met the required load in regular semesters receive remuneration for the summer semester; any deficiency is expected to be fulfilled through course offerings in the summer [C1].
*   **Specific High-Load Roles:**  
    *   **HoDs and Journal EICs:** Assigned a fixed **3 CH** equivalent load [C9].
    *   **Top Prolific Researchers:** The Head of Institution (HoI) has discretion to assign up to **3 CHs** equivalent load as institutional service to the top 5% prolific researchers [C9].
    *   **Pro Rector (Academics):** Can assign up to **3 CHs** equivalent load per semester to any faculty for specialized assignments [C9].

### 3. Research Load Component
The research load component varies significantly based on PhD status, supervision duties, and funding levels.

*   **General Range:** The research load varies from **0 to 6 CHs** equivalent per semester [C11].
*   **PhD Supervision:**  
    *   Supervisor: **1 CH** per student per semester [C11].
    *   Co-supervisor: **0.5 CH** per student per semester [C11].
*   **MS/MBA Supervision:** **0.75 CH** per student per semester [C11].
*   **Funding (CHEL):** The load depends on the funding amount credited to NUST and the role:
    *   Principal Investigator (PI): Ranges from **1 to 3 CHs** depending on funding (up to >10M) [C11].
    *   Co-PI: Ranges from **0.5 to 1.5 CHs** [C11].
    *   Member: Ranges from **0.25 to 0.75 CHs** [C11].
