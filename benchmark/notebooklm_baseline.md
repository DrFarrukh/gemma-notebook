## Q1

Here is what the NUST Leave Policy specifies regarding **Ex-Pakistan Leave (EPL)** for faculty members:

### 1\. Overview of Ex-Pakistan Leave (EPL)

* **Definition:** Ex-Pakistan Leave is granted when an employee applies to travel abroad during leave or takes leave while on official duty abroad[1].
* **Duration:** It starts on the day you leave office and ends upon resuming duty, up to a maximum of **30 days at a time**[1].
* **Limit:** The leave period cannot exceed your available **Earned Leave (EL)** balance, up to a maximum of 30 days[2].
* **Sanctioning Authority:** As an Associate Professor (faculty), your Ex-Pakistan Leave is sanctioned by the **Pro-Rector (Academics)**[3]. The application is processed through your Head of Department (HoD), Dean, and Principal/Commandant before being sent to the HR Directorate[4].

### 2\. Can You Take Ex-Pakistan Leave While on Probation?

* **Against Earned Leave:** The policy does not explicitly restrict taking Ex-Pakistan Leave during probation, as long as you have accrued sufficient **Earned Leave balance** (earned at 2 days per month)[1] and obtain approval from the sanctioning authority[3].
* **As Extraordinary Leave (EOL):** If you do not have enough leave balance and need to take **Extraordinary Leave (EOL)** without pay for foreign travel, the policy explicitly states that you **must have been removed from probation** to avail EOL[8].

### 3\. Is It "Leave With Pay" or "Leave Without Pay"?

* **Leave With Pay:** It is **with full pay** if taken as Ex-Pakistan Leave against your accrued **Earned Leave (EL)** balance[1].
* **Leave Without Pay:** If your leave exceeds your available Earned Leave balance or is processed under **Extraordinary Leave (EOL)**, it is **without pay/emoluments**[9] (and requires completion of your probation period[8]).

## Q2

If you are planning a **2-year long leave out of Pakistan**, the NUST Leave Policy sets the following rules:

### 1\. Type of Leave

Standard Ex-Pakistan Leave is capped at a maximum of **30 days at a time**[1][2]. To take a continuous 2-year leave abroad (e.g., for research, a post-doc, or personal reasons), it falls under **Extraordinary Leave (EOL)**, which can be granted for up to **2 years at a stretch**[3].

### 2\. Can You Take It While on Probation?

**No.** To be eligible for Extraordinary Leave (EOL), the policy explicitly states that you **must have been removed from probation**[4].

*(Note: If the long leave were for higher studies under Study Leave, NUST similarly mandates a strict minimum of* **2 years of completed service** *before you can apply*[5]*).*

### 3\. Is It "Leave With Pay" or "Leave Without Pay"?

It is **leave without pay**[3]. EOL is granted without pay or emoluments, and no annual increments accrue during this period[3][6].

## Q4

According to the NUST Faculty Load Policy, the minimum and maximum workload expectations per regular semester are structured as follows:

### **1\. Overall Faculty Load**

* **Minimum Overall Load:** **12 Credit Hours (CHs)** per regular semester, combining teaching, research, and institutional services[1]. *(Note: For newly joined PhD faculty, this minimum is reduced to* **8 CHs** *during their first year of service)*[1]*.*
* **Maximum Overall Load:** While there is no explicit total cap on overall load, limits apply to individual components (research and administrative duties) as detailed below[2][3].

### **2\. Teaching Load**

* **Minimum Mandatory Teaching:** At least **3 CHs** of actual classroom teaching per semester[4].
* **Teaching Load Formula:** Required Teaching Load = `12 CHs − Research Load − Institutional Services Load`[4].

### **3\. Component Limits (Research &amp; Institutional Services)**

* **Research Load Limit:** **0 to 6 CH** equivalent per semester[2] *(earned through graduate student supervision, grants, and publication activities)*[2][5].
* **Institutional Services Limit:** Up to a maximum of **3 CH** equivalent[3] *(or up to* **4 CH** *if serving as an Associate Dean or Director of Academics)*[3][6].
* **Shared Faculty / Extra Load:** Up to **3 CHs extra** can be taken as Shared Faculty across other NUST departments beyond your primary appointment load[7].
